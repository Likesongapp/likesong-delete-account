Delete your LikeSong account

Users can delete their LikeSong account directly in the app, or request account deletion without access to the app.

Delete in the app

  1. Open LikeSong.
  2. Go to Profile / Settings.
  3. Choose Delete account.
  4. Confirm deletion.

Request deletion without the app

If you cannot access the LikeSong app, you can request deletion of your account by email. You do not need to sign in, open the app, or reinstall it to send a request.

Request account deletion

The button opens an email draft. Enter the email address used for your LikeSong account and send the email to submit your request.

If no email app opens, use your preferred email service to write to likesong.support@gmail.com with the subject LikeSong account deletion request and include the email address used for your account.

What happens when your account is deleted

  • Your profile, including its stored personal information, is deleted.
  • Friend requests and friendship records involving your account are deleted.
  • Private messages sent or received by your account are deleted.
  • Your club chat messages are deleted.
  • Your song votes are deleted.
  • Your user presence and online-status record are deleted.
  • Your club posts are deleted.
  • Your account-linked push token registrations and blocks involving your account are deleted.
  • Your account authentication record is deleted.
  • Your account-owned avatar files are deleted. Club post images are removed when ownership can be verified; unverified legacy images may remain.

Some content may remain without its original account link:

  • Songs suggested by you may remain in clubs, but your user ID and avatar are removed and the author is shown as Deleted user.
  • Clubs you created may remain, but your account is no longer linked as their owner.
  • Moderation and report records may remain. References to your user ID as the reporter or reported user are removed where applicable.

Verifying your request

For security, we may need to verify that the request comes from the owner of the LikeSong account before deletion is completed.

Please do not include your password or verification codes in your deletion request.
